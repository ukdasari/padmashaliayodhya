Our Vision for Ayodhya

Continuing our service to the Padmashali community

Ayodhya, the birthplace of Lord Shri Rama, is rapidly becoming one of the world's most prominent spiritual and tourist destinations. We are establishing the Seventh Padmashali Anna Satram here so every devotee from our community has a place to stay and a meal to share.

What is a Sthala Daata?

A Sthala Daata is a land donor who contributes ₹1,50,000 toward the Ayodhya Satram. Membership is limited to 1,000 people and is hereditary.

What benefits do Sthala Daatas receive?

Seven days of free accommodation and food every year in the regular rooms, plus holiday home accommodation at concessional rates.

Does membership pass on to my family?

Yes. The membership is hereditary and continues for the successors of the Sthala Daata.

What has been done so far?

2.5 acres have been acquired through an Agreement of Sale, and a boundary wall has been built around the property.
